FAQ

Questions, answered

What is RoboAgent?+

6,666 robot AI agents on BNB Chain, in the style of AgentCoin — but simplified. No .ai name needed to mint, no proof-of-work puzzle, no dollar conversion. First mint FREE, then 0.0005 / 0.001 / 0.002 BNB, max 4 per wallet.

How does the free window work?+

The first mint per wallet is FREE for 3 days from launch — or until the 1,111 free mints run out, whichever comes first. After 3 days the free mint closes, but paid minting stays open until all 6,666 agents are minted.

How much does a mint cost?+

Mint #1 is FREE (during the free window). Mint #2 costs 0.0005 BNB, #3 costs 0.001 BNB, #4 costs 0.002 BNB. Max 4 agents per wallet, max 4 per transaction. You only ever pay BNB gas on top.

What is fusing?+

Burn 3 Genesis agents you own to forge 1 Miner. The Miner earns 4x dividends (weight 4 vs Genesis weight 1) and gets a new token ID. The 3 Genesis are gone forever — fusing cannot be undone.

How do dividends work?+

40% of every paid mint goes into the dividend pool, 60% goes to the treasury (the collection owner). Holders claim from the pool by weight — Genesis 1, Miner 4 — any time. Dividends are shared mint fees, not a promised profit: if nobody mints paid, the pool stays empty.
